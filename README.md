# AI Adoption Health Check

A single-page web application that helps team leads diagnose their team's AI adoption maturity through an anonymous survey, research-backed benchmarks, and a 90-day action plan.

## Features

- **Anonymous Team Survey** — 10-minute assessment covering tool usage, workflow integration, prompting habits, and organizational support
- **Adoption Spectrum** — Classifies respondents as Power Users, Early Adopters, or Non-Adopters based on survey responses
- **Research-Backed Benchmarks** — Compares your team's results against data from Gensler 2026 (16,400 workers), ResumeTemplates (1,000+ workers), and UC Berkeley Haas (200 employees)
- **Risk Signals** — Highlights areas of concern such as low adoption, shadow AI usage, or integration gaps
- **90-Day Action Plan** — Generates a phased plan tailored to your team's diagnostic results
- **Monthly Pulse** — Track adoption progress over time
- **Dark/Light Theme** — Toggle between dark and light modes
- **QR Code Sharing** — Share the survey link with your team via QR code
- **PDF Export** — Export diagnostic results for stakeholders
- **Fully Client-Side** — No backend required; all data is stored in the browser via localStorage

## Getting Started

1. Open `index.html` in a browser
2. Enter your team name, size, and industry
3. Share the generated survey link with your team
4. View the diagnostic dashboard once 3+ responses are collected

## Tech Stack

- Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript (no build step or dependencies)
- Google Fonts (Fraunces, DM Sans)
